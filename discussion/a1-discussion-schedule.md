# A1 Discussion Schedule — Compressed Block

With 15 groups meeting Mon/Thu, the discussion for each project runs across **two meetings** rather than four. Every group still gets a live slot; the block simply closes while the work is still fresh instead of trailing two weeks behind it.

## How it works

- **Two meetings per project, 7–8 groups each.** Longer discussion rounds, but the whole cohort is covered within a week of the deadline instead of across a fortnight. Under the old 3–4-per-session rotation, the last groups presented work they had finished ten days earlier, with the next project already underway.
- **Slot length:** 5 min presentation + 2 min Q&A.
- **No written recaps.** Every group presents live every cycle, so the recap file is not required. (It never was in practice — the old rule asked for one only from groups *not* spotlighted, and the rotation spotlighted all 15.)
- **Discussion starts at the meeting a project's deadline falls before.**
- **"S" numbers are curriculum sessions, not meetings.** A session can run across two meetings, or share one with a discussion round. Everything below is keyed on dates.

## Where the cohort actually is

**Session numbers are curriculum sessions, not calendar dates.** A session can span more than one meeting, and a meeting can be split between discussion and new material — so the two do not line up one-to-one, and this schedule is keyed on **dates**.

## Mentor QA timing

Reviews happen in the gap between the QA cutoff and the final deadline, so every group can act on the feedback before it is graded — not after.

| | Project 1 | Rule for later projects |
|---|---|---|
| **QA cutoff** — groups push their working version | Fri 28/08/2026 | 3 days before the final deadline |
| **Mentor reviews posted** — all 15 groups | Sun 30/08/2026 | 1 day before the final deadline |
| **Final deadline** — fixes pushed | Mon 31/08/2026 | the project's first discussion round |

All four mentors review in the same window, roughly 4 groups each, regardless of when their batch is spotlighted — a Batch 4 group presenting in the last round still gets its review by 30/08, because it still has to hit the same final deadline. The staggered spotlight is presentation order only, not review order.

Mentor-to-batch assignments are in `qa/a1-mentor-rotation.md`.
